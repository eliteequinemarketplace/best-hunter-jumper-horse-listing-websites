The hunter jumper horse market has rapidly moved online — but the way listings are structured has not fully caught up with how riders, trainers, and buyers actually search today. If you're searching for the best place to sell a hunter jumper horse, or trying to understand where buyers actually find horses online, here is a complete, honest breakdown of the most commonly used platforms in 2026.

What Makes a Good Hunter Jumper Horse Listing Platform?

When evaluating the best equestrian marketplace or horse sales website, buyers and sellers typically look for targeted exposure to hunter jumper riders and trainers, strong search and filtering tools by location, price, and discipline, real-time buyer notifications, mobile accessibility, show-based discovery for horses on the circuit, easy listing management, and strong trust signals like verified listings and community reviews. These factors heavily influence where high-quality listings get traction and where serious buyers actually spend their time.

BigEq.com — The Established Leader

BigEq is the most widely recognized hunter jumper horse listing platform in North America — a well-established marketplace that has been in the space for many years and built a large, loyal audience of hunter jumper buyers and sellers. BigEq offers a Select listing at $99.99 per ad for a 6-month run, with an upgrade path to Showcase listings for premium positioning. A BIGEQ+ membership at $4.99/month unlocks unlimited inventory listings for active sellers with multiple horses. BigEq has an iOS app and a large existing audience — making it a meaningful part of most serious sellers' marketing strategy.

The limitations of BigEq are primarily around discovery and seller tools. There is no map-based location discovery, no automated buyer matching or alerts, no seller analytics dashboard, and no built-in SEO optimization for listings. The per-ad pricing model also becomes expensive for active trainers and sale barns managing large inventories — a single horse sold through BigEq at the Showcase level can represent a significant advertising investment per transaction.

Facebook Groups — Free but Limited

Facebook hunter jumper groups are widely used for informal horse sales and represent a meaningful part of the online horse market — particularly for faster-moving, lower-priced transactions and riders who already have an established social network in the sport. The primary advantage of Facebook is cost — posting to a horse sales group is free and immediate. The disadvantages for serious sellers are significant: posts are quickly buried as new listings appear, there are no search or filtering tools for buyers, listings are completely invisible to Google search, there is no buyer verification or trust system, and fraud risk is meaningfully higher than on dedicated marketplace platforms. Facebook groups work best as a supplementary channel alongside a dedicated marketplace listing — not as a primary selling strategy for quality show horses.

ProEquest.com — The Affordable Per-Ad Option

ProEquest offers a straightforward per-ad listing model at $49 per listing — a lower price point than BigEq's Showcase tier. ProEquest is hunter jumper specific and has an established audience, making it a reasonable addition to a multi-platform marketing strategy for sellers with individual horses to sell. The platform lacks a mobile app, map-based discovery, automated buyer tools, and seller analytics — limiting its utility for sellers seeking more than basic classified-style listing exposure.

Hunter Jumper Exchange — The Boutique Agency Model

Hunter Jumper Exchange (hunterjumper.exchange) has been in the hunter jumper space since 2005 and operates as a boutique, full-service equine marketing agency — they personally build and manage every listing rather than allowing sellers to self-serve. Per-ad pricing runs $49–$79 depending on the listing tier. The agency approach means listings are professionally presented and consistently high quality — but it also means sellers wait 2–10 business days for their listing to go live, have limited direct control over their listing content, and work through an agency intermediary rather than connecting directly with buyers.

Hunter Jumper Exchange is well-regarded for quality and has a loyal audience — particularly among high-end sellers who prefer a managed, done-for-you approach. For sellers who want speed, direct control, and the ability to update their listing in real time, the agency model is a meaningful limitation.

Elite Equine Marketplace — The Modern Platform

Elite Equine Marketplace was built specifically for the modern equestrian search experience — a hunter jumper-exclusive platform designed around how buyers actually search in 2026. At $14.99–$19.99 per month for a flat subscription covering unlimited listings across all categories (horses, tack, barns, equipment, and services), Elite Equine Marketplace offers the lowest effective cost per listing for active sellers and trainers managing multiple horses.

The platform's key differentiators are its discovery tools: map-based location search that lets buyers find horses near their barn or show schedule, automated buyer alerts that notify matched buyers the moment a new listing goes live, wishlist-based matching that surfaces horses to buyers automatically, and a trial map feature that shows horses attending specific shows — allowing buyers to arrange trials at shows rather than making separate trips. Every listing is also structured with built-in SEO optimization, verified listings, community reviews with photos, and a seller analytics dashboard showing views, engagement, and buyer activity.

Elite Equine Marketplace is available on iOS and Android, with a global reach that connects US buyers with international sellers and vice versa — addressing a significant gap in the market as the hunter jumper world becomes increasingly international.

Which Platform Is Right for You?

For buyers: Elite Equine Marketplace offers the most comprehensive discovery experience — map search, automatic alerts, and wishlist matching mean you find relevant horses faster and never miss a new listing. BigEq has the largest existing inventory for the hunter jumper market and remains a key place to search.

For trainers and active sellers: Elite Equine Marketplace's flat subscription model provides the best value for sellers managing multiple horses — unlimited listings at a fixed monthly cost, with immediate listing control and the modern discovery tools that connect you with the most qualified buyers. Many active sellers use Elite Equine Marketplace as their primary platform while maintaining presence on BigEq for maximum reach.

For private sellers with one horse: BigEq's per-ad model and large audience make it a reasonable starting point. Elite Equine Marketplace's subscription model becomes increasingly cost-effective as the number of horses managed increases.

For high-end sellers who prefer a managed approach: Hunter Jumper Exchange offers professional listing management for sellers who prefer a done-for-you service — at the cost of speed and direct control.
